`clans.json` is generated by `scripts/pull-data.ts` (`bun run pull-data`) from:

- **English Wikipedia**: [List of Scottish clans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Scottish_clans) and each clan article's `{{Infobox clan}}`. Text is licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Credit Wikipedia and link to the articles (`wikipediaUrl`).
- **Wikidata**: inception, seat coordinates and notable people. Licensed under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Tartan threadcounts**: [thetartan/tartan-database](https://github.com/thetartan/tartan-database) (formerly `kravets-levko/tartan-database`), commit `55adceb` (data last updated 2017-01-10). Downloaded at run time into `.cache/`; nothing is vendored.
